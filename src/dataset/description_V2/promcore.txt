Task: Binary classification to identify core promoter regions in human DNA, which regulate transcription initiation near the transcription start site (TSS). Labels indicate whether a sequence is a promoter (True) or non-promoter (False). Promoter sequences are extracted as a 70-bp window (-34 to +35 bp relative to the TSS) from the Eukaryotic Promoter Database (EPDnew) (Dreos et al., 2013), partitioned into TATA (containing TATA box motifs) and non-TATA classes. Non-promoters are rigorously designed to minimize bias: TATA-containing negatives are sampled from non-promoter genomic regions with TATA motifs, while non-TATA negatives are generated via dinucleotide-shuffled substitutions to retain background sequence properties. A combined dataset (all) merges both classes. Focused on the minimal functional core promoter, this task challenges models to recognize subtle regulatory signals (e.g., TATA box, initiator motifs) within a short sequence window, aiding studies of transcriptional dysregulation in diseases (e.g., cancer, developmental disorders) and enabling precision editing of synthetic promoters
