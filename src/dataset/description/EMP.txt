Task: Binary classification to predict histone modification presence (True/False) in yeast DNA sequences. Labels indicate whether a histone modification is present (True) or not (False).This dataset is designed to  Histone modifications (e.g., acetylation, methylation) regulate gene expression by altering chromatin structure without changing DNA. The dataset comprises 10 independent collections, each split into 80% training, 10% validation, and 10% test sets. Labels indicate sequence-level associations with specific modifications. Predicting these marks aids in identifying functional non-coding regions and understanding epigenetic regulation mechanisms. The task leverages supervised learning to map sequence features to histone modification states, enabling genome-wide annotation and hypothesis generation for yeast epigenetics research.
