Task: Binary classification to predict transcription factor binding sites (TFBS) in mouse DNA sequences. Labels indicate whether a sequence binds a specific transcription factor (True) or represents a non-binding control (False). The dataset derives from the mouse ENCODE Project (Stamatoyannopoulos et al., 2012), comprising 78 ChIP-seq-derived TFBS datasets hosted on the UCSC Genome Browser. To ensure robustness, 5 datasets were randomly selected and processed identically to the human TFBS benchmark. Negative examples were generated via dinucleotide shuffling, preserving sequence composition (e.g., GC content, dimer frequencies) to minimize bias while ensuring no true binding activity. Training, validation, and test splits mirror the human dataset’s structure (likely 80:10:10, though unspecified). Predicting TFBS in mice aids in decoding gene regulatory networks, comparative genomics, and identifying conserved regulatory elements across species.
